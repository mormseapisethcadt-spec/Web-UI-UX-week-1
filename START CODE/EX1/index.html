Khmer New Year, locally known as Choul Chnam Thmey (ចូលឆ្នាំថ្មី), is Cambodia's largest and most cherished three-day national holiday, usually celebrated from April 13 to 16. It marks the end of the traditional solar harvest season and the transition into the new year.
